# Findings

**12,330 sessions** contain **1,908 conversions**, an overall rate of **15.47%**. Among visitor groups with at least 100 sessions, **New_Visitor** has the highest observed conversion rate (24.91%; n=1,694).

Visitor type has an observed association with conversion: Cramer's V=0.105, chi-square p=4.27e-30. This comparison is descriptive. It does not establish that changing visitor type changes conversion, or that any channel has a causal effect. The p-value does not adjust for the other exploratory segment tables.

Recommendation: use the scorecard to identify hypotheses for a separately randomised experiment. Check audience mix, acquisition source, and season before changing budgets. Revenue is a Boolean label: this analysis cannot estimate sales value, ROAS, or profit. Month categories are not complete dates and cannot support chronological retention cohorts.

All SQL segment totals reconcile to the original row and conversion totals. Wilson intervals quantify sampling uncertainty under independent-session assumptions; they do not correct selection or measurement bias.
