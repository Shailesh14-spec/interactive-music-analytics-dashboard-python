The downloader extracts `bank-additional/bank-additional-full.csv` from the nested archive bank-additional.zip, verifies its SHA-256, and saves the original bytes without changes. Raw data is not committed. Analysis-derived aggregate tables and charts are adaptations of this dataset; feature engineering and exclusions are described in the README and code.
